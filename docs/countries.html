Figure 5: National specialisation

Revealed comparative advantage (RCA) compares a country's share of quantum activity in one technology domain with that domain's share of quantum activity worldwide. An RCA above 1.0 means the country is more specialised in that domain than the global average; below 1.0, less. It measures relative emphasis, not size — a small ecosystem can score highly in a domain it concentrates on.
