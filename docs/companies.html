Explore the highest-funded quantum companies worldwide, ranked by total funding. Use the pillar selector to view the top companies across the five quantum technology pillars, and the range buttons to browse the ranking 10 companies at a time.

Figure 5: Top 50 parent institutions by spinout activity

Compare institutions by the number of spinouts they have produced or by the total funding raised by those spinouts. Use the toggle to switch between rankings and the range buttons to browse the ranking 10 institutions at a time.

Only companies originating as university, research, corporate or hybrid spinouts are included. Where a spinout has more than one institution of origin, it is counted under each, with its funding attributed to each parent institution.
