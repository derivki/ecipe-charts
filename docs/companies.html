Parent institutions by funding raised by their spinouts

Institutions ranked by the total funding raised by the companies that came out of them — not by how many they produced. Use the toggle to compare the two.

A company with more than one institution of origin counts under each, so funding is deliberately counted for every co-parent — the question is what came out of an institution, not how to divide the sector up.
