Figure 1: Top 30 quantum companies by funding

The highest-funded quantum companies worldwide, ranked by company funding. A technology pillar is one of the five technology domains, such as quantum computing or quantum sensing; choose one to see its top companies.

The ranking reflects cumulative funding and may include companies that are now defunct, have been acquired, or have merged with other firms. Companies are classified here by their primary quantum technology pillar. Additional pillar classifications may apply but are not shown in the ranking.

How many of the tracked companies were founded in each year, showing how recent the sector's company base is.

Figure 3: Funding across the quantum technology landscape

How funding is distributed across technology pillars (the five technology domains), stack layers (position in the value chain, from materials to applications) and streams (the specific product category within a layer).

Hover over a bar to see the companies it contains.

Companies carry a single stack-layer classification. For technology pillars and streams, multiple classifications may apply, but companies are shown here according to their primary pillar and primary stream.

Figure 4: How companies are founded

How the tracked companies came into being: out of an institution (as a university, research or corporate spinout, a hybrid spinout, a joint venture, a merger or a subsidiary) or founded independently.

Figure 5: Top 50 parent institutions by spinout activity

Parent institutions ranked by the number of spinouts they have produced or by the company funding those spinouts have raised. A spinout is a company that originated from a university, research organisation or corporation.

Only companies originating as university, research, corporate or hybrid spinouts are included. Where a spinout has more than one institution of origin, it is counted under each, with its funding attributed to each parent institution.
