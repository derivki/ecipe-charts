Figure 6: Country collaboration network

Countries linked by cross-border collaborations between the institutions and companies in the tracker.

Each collaboration is one pair of institutions working together, counted once however many publications or announcements support it, and attributed to the two institutions' countries. Domestic collaborations are not shown. About of all collaborations are academic co-authorships, so the All collaborations view mostly maps scientific cooperation; the Involving industry view is the commercial picture.

Node colour is betweenness: how often a country lies on the shortest routes between other countries, where countries that collaborate more are closer. A dark node is a connector that links parts of the network that would otherwise be further apart. Line colour is bilateral RCA: the number of collaborations between two countries relative to what their overall activity would predict, so a dark line is a partnership stronger than average and a value of 1 is exactly as expected. Pairs with fewer than 3 collaborations, and countries with fewer than 10 in total, are not scored. Each view has its own betweenness and RCA, measured on that view's full network. Node size is the share of the other countries on screen that a country is linked to.
