The global quantum landscape

Quantum technology is moving from the lab to the market. This tracker follows the money: announced funding, company formation and international collaboration across the global quantum ecosystem. Pick a layer below to drill into a country or an innovation cluster.

Public vs. private funding by country — world map

Shading = funding by country. Use Public / Private to switch between government-sourced funding (grants + public equity) and private capital (VC / private equity + debt). Scroll/drag to zoom.

Country shading is computed from the funding database. The underlying world map is a 110m-resolution atlas, so a few very small states (e.g. Singapore) have no polygon to shade. Clusters are shown on the Clusters tab →.
