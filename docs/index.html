Quantum Technologies Tracker

Explore the companies building quantum technologies and the institutions researching them. Trace private and public funding across every segment of the industry. Compare national ecosystems and innovation clusters, and the collaborations that link them within and across borders. Free, interactive, and updated quarterly by ECIPE.

Figure 1: Global distribution of company vs. government funding

Shading indicates funding by country. Use Company / Government to switch between total funding raised by companies and total funding announced or deployed by government entities. Scroll or drag to zoom.

Company and government funding should not be added together: Government already includes money counted once as company funding, wherever a government provides grants, acquires equity stakes directly, or participates in a broader financing round, so the two categories overlap by construction. The underlying world map is a 110m-resolution atlas, so a few very small states (e.g. Singapore) have no polygon to shade.

Figure 2: Global company funding by year and financing instrument

Total funding by year, broken down by instrument: VC / private equity, debt, grant, public equity. Use Measure to switch between funding amount and percentage of annual funding, and Chart type to switch between bars and area. Click an instrument in the legend to show or hide it.

Chart starts at ; pre- funding is negligible and is therefore not shown. Figures for the most recent year cover funding recorded through the latest available quarter; previous years represent full calendar years.

Figure 3: Countries ranked by company vs. government funding

Use Company / Government to switch the metric and the range buttons to browse the ranking 10 countries at a time.

The figure includes both the EU as a whole and individual EU Member States.

Figure 4: Top companies by total funding

Total funding refers to funding from all instruments: VC / private equity, debt, grant, public equity. Explore the Companies tab →

The ranking reflects cumulative funding and may include companies that are now defunct, have been acquired, or have merged with other firms. See the Companies tab for the full ranking.

Figure 5: Capital raised by funding stage and region

Compare quantum funding across stages in two regions. Choose the regions to compare and use Measure to switch between funding amount and percentage of each region's total.

Late-stage and growth equity are combined; debt and grants are excluded from the funding-stage comparison.
