About this tracker

What this is

A quarterly, freely available record of funding, company formation and collaboration across the global quantum technology sector, published by ECIPE and computed directly from the underlying data rather than maintained by hand.

Data & methodology

Every published figure is computed directly from the underlying tables — a company register and a funding-rounds ledger — rather than maintained by hand, so it can be reproduced and re-checked each quarter.

Company funding is all capital raised by the tracked companies, across every instrument (VC and private equity, debt, grants, public equity). Government funding is a separate measure: money announced or deployed through national and supranational quantum policy programmes, sourced from ECIPE's own policy register. It does not include grants or public equity that reached companies through funding rounds — that money is counted only once, under Company funding.

Panels marked Illustrative · mock data use placeholder figures pending publication of the underlying analysis and should not be cited. Government funding figures are currently marked Provisional for a different reason: the numbers are real, but the treatment of overlapping multi-year programmes is not yet settled, so national totals for the largest funders are upper bounds.
