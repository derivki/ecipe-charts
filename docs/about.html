About this tracker

What the Quantum Technologies Tracker covers, how it is built, and how to read it.

What this is

A short introduction to the project.

The Quantum Technologies Tracker follows announced funding, company formation and international collaboration across the global quantum computing and quantum technologies ecosystem. It is built and maintained by ECIPE (the European Centre for International Political Economy) from a proprietary database of quantum startups and their funding rounds, and is updated quarterly.

Data & methodology

How the figures on this site are produced.

Every published figure is computed directly from two underlying tables — a company register and a funding-rounds ledger — rather than maintained by hand, so it can be reproduced and re-checked each quarter. Public funding means grants and public equity (government-sourced instruments); private funding means VC/private equity and debt. Panels marked Illustrative · mock data use placeholder figures pending publication of the underlying analysis and should not be cited.
