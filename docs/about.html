About this tracker

What this is

Explore the companies building quantum technologies and the institutions researching them. Trace private and public funding across every segment of the industry. Compare national ecosystems and innovation clusters, and the collaborations that link them within and across borders. Free, interactive, and updated quarterly by ECIPE.

Data & methodology

Every published figure is computed directly from two underlying tables — a company register and a funding-rounds ledger — rather than maintained by hand, so it can be reproduced and re-checked each quarter. Public funding means grants and public equity (government-sourced instruments); private funding means VC/private equity and debt. Panels marked Illustrative · mock data use placeholder figures pending publication of the underlying analysis and should not be cited.
