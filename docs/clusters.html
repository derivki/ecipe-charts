Table 1: 2025 cluster ranking

The overall rank is the average of each cluster's ranking across three dimensions: Market Orientation (commercialisation), Collaboration Intensity (partnerships and connectivity) and Ecosystem Maturity (institutional strength and productivity). Clusters with the same score share a rank, marked = as in the paper. 1 indicates the highest-ranked cluster. This is the 2025 index; the 2026 ranking will follow.
